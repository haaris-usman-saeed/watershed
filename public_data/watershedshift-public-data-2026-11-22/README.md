# WatershedShift public data, version 2026-11-22

Derived weather inputs only. Each file's sources, field definitions, missingness and checksum are in MANIFEST.json. This release is immutable; corrections are published as a new version.

Not included until the California Department of Water Resources confirms redistribution terms:

- CDEC daily storage (sensor 15) and any table containing it
- CDEC snow water content (sensor 82) and the basin snow index
- weekly feature tables, forecasts, residuals and model artifacts (fitted to CDEC values)

Attribution:

- NOAA GEFSv12 reforecast (Guan et al. 2022, Monthly Weather Review, doi:10.1175/MWR-D-21-0245.1), via the NOAA Open Data Dissemination program on AWS. Retrospective forecasts, not issued in real time. Derived by WatershedShift; no NOAA endorsement is implied.
- NOAA NCEP Global Ensemble Forecast System version 12 (GEFSv12), via the NOAA Open Data Dissemination program on AWS. Basin aggregates are derived by WatershedShift and are not unaltered NOAA data; no NOAA endorsement is implied.
- The data was obtained from the National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA) Langley Research Center (LaRC) Prediction of Worldwide Energy Resources (POWER) Project funded through the NASA Earth Science Directorate Applied Science Program.
