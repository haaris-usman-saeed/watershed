# Cross-check of WatershedShift storage against ResOpsUS

ResOpsUS: Steyaert, Condon, Turner and Voisin (2022), *Scientific Data* 9, 34, doi:10.1038/s41597-022-01134-7; Zenodo record 5893641 (ResOpsUS.zip, CC-BY 4.0; SHA-256 in data/raw_manifest.jsonl). Storage converted from million cubic meters to acre-feet (1 MCM = 810.7132 AF). Comparison window 2001-01-01 to 2020-12-31 (ResOpsUS ends about 2021-01-01). Our side: data/processed/daily_joined.csv after cleaning. Generated by `python -m src.compare_resopsus`.

Lag k means the ResOpsUS date is shifted by k days before matching (lag 0 = same calendar date). `match_share` is the share of common days that agree within 2 AF (rounding of the 3-decimal MCM values) plus 5e-6 x storage (ResOpsUS used a slightly different acre-foot to cubic-meter constant; SHA and NML differ by a near-constant 1.5e-6 relative).
