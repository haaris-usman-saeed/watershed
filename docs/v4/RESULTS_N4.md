### N4 as run (the selected configuration, `wb_noaux`)

| Part | Setting |
|---|---|
| Architecture | `WaterBalanceED` in `src/v4/nets.py`, 48,036 parameters |
| Encoder | LSTM over 30 days ending at the origin. Inputs: storage and CDEC inflow and outflow (sensors 76 and 23) up to the origin day, basin snow index to the day before, NASA POWER weather at the dam point and the upstream-box mean to 7 days before, each with a mask, plus day of year, a 4-dimensional reservoir embedding and log capacity |
| Decoder | LSTM over the 30 days after the origin; inputs are the calendar and the lead time only |
| Daily heads | Inflow, release (≥ 0) and loss (≥ 0) in percentage points of capacity, plus a residual bounded to ±1 pp |
| Water-balance layer | Integrates them into the daily storage change |
| Quantiles | 9 non-crossing quantiles; the median is the 30-day change |
| Loss | Pinball loss of the 30-day change, plus L1 on the daily storage path, plus a feasibility hinge, plus 0.5 × mean \|residual\| |
| Inflow and outflow loss terms | Weight 0 (that is what "noaux" means) |
| Size and training | Hidden 64, 1 layer, dropout 0.2; AdamW, learning rate 1e-3, weight decay 1e-4, batch 256, up to 30 epochs, patience 4 |
| Training data per fold | Every second daily origin with target before the fold's start; the last 365 days of origins form the early-stopping set |
| Seeds | 0, 1 and 2. "Ensemble" means the mean of the three seeds' quantiles |
| Weights | No model weights were saved, so nothing is frozen |

### The reference: "HGB SWE no weather", refit per fold

- **Estimator:** scikit-learn `HistGradientBoostingRegressor` with the frozen v2 hyperparameters: learning_rate 0.03, max_iter 150, max_depth None, min_samples_leaf 30, l2_regularization 1.0, random_state 42.
- **Inputs:** the 15 v2 inputs: storage %, 7/30/60-day storage changes, basin SWE, 14-day SWE change, SWE anomaly against a fold-specific climatology, season sine and cosine, and reservoir one-hots.
- **Training data:** refit for each water year W on v2 weekly rows with target date before Oct 1 of W−1.
- **This is not the frozen v2 artifact.**
- It reproduces the earlier `compare` stage exactly (3.1598 pp); the code asserts this.

### The owner's three conditions for showing v4 on the website

1. **N4 is built.** Yes.
2. **N4 beats the HGB SWE no weather refit on the development folds.** **Not established.**
   - N4's 3-seed ensemble has the lowest point MAE of any model on these rows: 3.084 against 3.160 pp.
   - The difference, −0.076 pp, has 95% CIs that span zero at every block length.
   - A single N4 run (one seed) averages 3.160 ± 0.055 pp, the same as HGB. The point advantage comes from averaging three seeds.
3. **N4 is frozen.** No. No weights were saved, and freezing is a separate owner decision (`PROTOCOL.md` §7).

**Under the standing decision, v4 stays off the website.**

## 3. What the water-balance structure does and does not do

- **The water-balance layer adds no measurable accuracy.** The same encoder-decoder with a direct head scores 3.092 pp, against 3.084 with the layer, a difference of −0.008 pp that is not established.
- **The selected model does not learn real inflow and release.**
  - Without inflow and outflow loss terms, the split of the predicted change into "inflow", "release" and "loss" is not identified by the data.
  - On the confirmation rows, its cumulative 30-day "inflow" is off by 8.97 pp on average against the operator-computed CDEC labels, and its "release" by 8.56 pp.
  - Its learned loss averages 0.42 pp per 30 days, larger than the 0.13–0.28 pp the flow audit suggests (`FLOW_DATA.md`).
  - **These components of `wb_noaux` must not be presented as inflow or release forecasts.**
- **With flow supervision the components become meaningful, but accuracy does not improve.**
  - `wb_strict` predicts cumulative inflow to within 4.00 pp and release to within 2.96 pp on average.
  - Its predicted change equals predicted inflow minus release and loss exactly. That is an accounting identity between predicted terms, not a guarantee about real water.
  - It scores 3.155 pp, level with HGB (−0.004, not established).
  - On the tuning folds, every flow-supervised candidate was worse than `wb_noaux` (2.40–2.43 against 2.26 pp).
- **The bounded residual is essentially unused.** The mean |r(30)| is 0.002 pp.

## 6. Robustness and compute

**Robustness.** These models were trained without source dropout. Removing a source at inference raises the MAE from 3.084:

| Source removed | MAE (pp) |
|---|---|
| Flows | 3.864 |
| Weather | 3.575 |
| Snow | 3.383 |

The weather sensitivity is larger than the N2 GRU's (3.306 without weather).

**Compute.** About 24 seconds per fold-seed on an Apple M5 CPU with 8 threads. The 72 confirmation runs took 29 minutes.

## 7. What this does and does not show

- It **does** show:
  - a working, tested, water-balance-structured encoder-decoder;
  - the lowest point MAE of any model tried on the 2016–2023 development folds;
  - a supported improvement over N1 and over the seasonal baseline.
- It does **not** show:
  - that N4 is more accurate than the HGB SWE no weather refit (not established);
  - that the water-balance layer helps;
  - anything about 2024–2026 or about real-time performance.
- **Real-time use is unverified.** N4 needs CDEC inflow and outflow (sensors 76 and 23) up to the forecast day. Their real-time availability has not been checked, and they are not covered by `config/data_release_policy.json`.

## Possible next steps (owner's choice; any selection on tuning folds only)

1. **Ensemble size.** The 3-seed ensemble is clearly better than single seeds: 3.084 against 3.160 pp on average. A larger ensemble, chosen on tuning folds, is the cheapest likely gain.
2. **Calibration.** Chronological conformal adjustment of the quantiles, compared on identical rows with HGB intervals.
3. **Source-dropout training,** and a check that sensors 76 and 23 are available in real time.
4. **Freezing.** If the owner wants N4 evaluated on genuinely future data, it must first be frozen (code hash, configuration, weights, seeds). The prospective protocol would then need a new version committed before N4's first official forecast (`docs/prospective/PROTOCOL.md` §1 and §7).
